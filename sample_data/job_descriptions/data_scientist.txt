Lead Data Scientist & Machine Learning Engineer

Company: Nexus Cognitive Systems
Location: San Francisco, CA / Remote
Experience Required: 3+ years in applied machine learning and data science
Education: Master's or PhD in Computer Science, Statistics, Mathematics, or related quantitative field

About the Role:
Nexus Cognitive Systems is looking for an experienced Data Scientist and Machine Learning Engineer to lead the development of our predictive modeling and explainable AI pipelines.

Key Responsibilities:
- Design and deploy machine learning and deep learning models for classification, ranking, and NLP tasks.
- Apply interpretability and Explainable AI frameworks, including SHAP and feature attribution methods, to explain complex models.
- Process large-scale tabular and unstructured text datasets using Pandas, NumPy, and Scikit-learn.
- Train transformer models and neural networks using PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- Partner with product and engineering teams to integrate ML models into production microservices and REST APIs.

Required Skills & Qualifications:
- 3+ years of professional experience in machine learning, statistics, and data science.
- Advanced proficiency in Python, Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn, and SQL.
- Strong hands-on experience with PyTorch or TensorFlow for deep learning and NLP.
- Practical experience with model interpretability and Explainable AI (SHAP, feature importance).
- Strong foundation in statistical modeling, hypothesis testing, and evaluation metrics (ROC-AUC, Precision, Recall, F1).
